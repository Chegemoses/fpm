About Us

Founder

Ezekiel Is a dedicated researcher specializing in climate, soil, and agriculture, with a strong focus on sustainable farming solutions. His work bridges the gap between scientific research and practical agricultural applications, ensuring that farmers can adapt to changing environmental conditions. Recognizing the need for an innovative approach to modern agricultural challenges, Ezekiel founded FPM, a pioneering system designed to revolutionize farming practices. Collaborating with his esteemed counterparts, Alice and Jacob, he aims to integrate cutting-edge research, soil science, and fertilizer technology to enhance agricultural productivity and sustainability. Through FPM, Ezekiel is leading efforts to create a more resilient and efficient farming ecosystem.



Co-Founder

Alice, a distinguished agricultural expert with extensive experience in the field. She has played a major role in agricultural research and development, contributing significantly to advancements in sustainable farming practices. As a senior lecturer at a top university in Melbourne, she has mentored countless students and conducted groundbreaking research in agriculture. Despite her academic commitments in Australia, Alice currently resides in Kenya, where she continues to engage in agricultural initiatives, collaborating with local farmers and institutions to improve food security and innovative farming techniques.

Co-Founder

Jacob, A renowned professor specializing in soil science, with a deep understanding of soil health, fertility, and conservation. His expertise has led to impactful research and innovations in soil management. In addition to his academic contributions, he is also an entrepreneur, leading a company that manufactures high-quality fertilizers designed to enhance soil productivity and support sustainable agriculture. Through his company, Jacob has helped farmers improve crop yields and adopt eco-friendly farming solutions, making him a key figure in the agricultural industry.
